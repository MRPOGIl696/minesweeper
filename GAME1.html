How to play
  • Type your username and pick a level.
  • Tap a tile to open it. The number shows how many bombs touch that tile.
  • Open every safe tile. Hit a bomb and you lose!
  • Use Flag mode 🚩 to mark tiles you think are bombs.
  • Beat the clock. Wait too long without tapping and the panic alarm starts. A safe tap calms it down.
  • Win to earn coins 🪙 and spend them in the Shop on new backgrounds.
